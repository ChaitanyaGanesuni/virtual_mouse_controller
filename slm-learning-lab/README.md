# SLM Learning Lab

An interactive, single-file lab for learning how a Small Language Model is built, from basic ideas to deployment.
Open `index.html` in any browser. It needs no build step and no GPU.

- Stages 0 (Understanding Language Models), 1 (Mathematical Foundations), 2 (Tokenization), 3 (Word Embeddings), 4 (Neural Network Fundamentals), 5 (Transformer Architecture), 6 (Building a Tiny Transformer), 7 (Dataset Preparation), 8 (Training the SLM), 9 (Model Evaluation), 10 (Fine-Tuning), 11 (Optimization & Quantization) and 12 (Deployment & Production) are fully built: the course is complete. Stage 1 covers tensors, the dot product, animated matrix multiplication, softmax, cross-entropy, derivatives, gradient descent on a loss landscape and step-by-step backpropagation, each with a "predict first" question.
- Later stages unlock when you score 4/5 or better on the previous assessment. Progress is saved in your browser.
- Every panel has a **Listen** button and every stage a **Narrated tour**. They use the browser's built-in speech engine in English, Telugu or Hindi, with captions, and play each panel's animation in step with the narration. If a language's voice isn't installed, the captions still play.

## SLM From Zero (beginner companion)

`slm-from-zero.html` is a gentle 10-stop tour for people with no AI background: one idea per stop, an everyday analogy (a bank trainee named Mira), a small interactive toy, a one-tap quiz and English read-aloud. Open it in any browser.
